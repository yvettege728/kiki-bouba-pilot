Red rings are the dot targets. Small dots are aligned gaze during each target, one colour per target. Each cluster should sit on its ring.

5. Per-trial metrics

ttff = time to first fixation from stimulus onset (ms). dwell = total fixation time (ms) before the response. Regions: target / other (rounds 1 and 2), spiky / round half of hybrid shapes, shape, and the two houses (round 3).
